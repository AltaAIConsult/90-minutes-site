Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information 90 Minutes or More collects when you visit 90minutesormore.com, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, such as your email address when you vote in a Stadium Zone poll or the Player of the Season awards, details you send through our contact form, and the shipping and contact details needed to fulfil a shop order. Payments are processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your full card details.

We also collect standard, anonymous usage data (such as pages visited, browser type, and approximate location) to understand how the site is used and to keep it running well.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to record votes and send vote confirmations, respond to your messages, process and ship orders, and improve our content and services. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted providers to operate the site, including Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), Printful (order fulfilment), Resend (email delivery), and Google (advertising). These providers only process your information as needed to deliver their services.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@90minutesormore.com.
